# Problem Statement

## Background & Context

Hostel environments accommodating thousands of students suffer from frequent maintenance breakdowns across electrical, plumbing, WiFi, furniture, cleaning, water supply, and security services.

Existing manual register-based systems suffer from severe pain points:
- **Lack of Transparency**: Students have no mechanism to track whether their complaint has been assigned or resolved.
- **Priority Mismanagement**: Emergency critical hazards (e.g., electrical short circuits or water line bursts) get queued behind routine cleaning requests.
- **No Accountability**: Wardens cannot measure staff resolution efficiency or response times.
- **Duplicate Submissions**: Multiple students in shared rooms submit duplicate complaints for the same issue, overloading staff registers.
- **Unchecked Delays**: Complaints remain pending indefinitely without automatic escalation or alert triggers.

## Proposed Solution

The **Smart Hostel Complaint Management System** solves these challenges by implementing:
- Centralized role-based access control (Student, Warden, Staff).
- Algorithmic priority classification and duplicate detection.
- Automated SLA escalation tracking.
- Interactive performance metrics and hotspot room analytics.
